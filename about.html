Who We Are

Founded in 2024

SupplyOpt is a decision science consulting firm founded by alumni of IIT Bombay. The company is built to deliver measurable business outcomes, not theoretical models. Our core team comes from the Industrial Engineering and Operations Research department at IIT Bombay and brings years of hands-on experience solving operational problems across manufacturing, supply chain, logistics, and planning functions.

What differentiates SupplyOpt is not just where we come from, but what our work delivers on the ground. We specialize in turning complex operational systems into clear, actionable decision frameworks that directly improve cost, throughput, service levels, and profitability.

We work with organizations facing real constraints such as limited capacity, volatile demand, tight service-level commitments, and high operating costs. By combining rigorous analytical methods with practical execution, we help leadership teams move from intuition-driven decisions to repeatable, data-backed actions that scale.

Our Vision

To help organizations achieve world-class operational performance by making the best use of their existing resources through reliable, data-driven decision systems.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: help businesses perform better using data-driven decision systems that create visible impact. Through our engagements, clients typically achieve:

  • Higher throughput and better utilization of existing capacity without capital expansion
  • Lower operating costs through optimized labor, inventory, and resource allocation
  • Improved service levels and delivery reliability under demand uncertainty
  • Reduced dependence on manual planning, firefighting, and ad-hoc decision-making

Our solutions span forecasting, optimization, simulation, and scenario analysis, designed specifically for real-world operating environments. We do not deliver static reports. We build decision systems that teams can use daily to plan, respond, and improve.

SupplyOpt works closely with manufacturing plants, warehouses, and transportation organizations to create resilient, scalable operations. The result is faster decisions, lower risk, and sustained performance improvement across cost, time, and service metrics.
